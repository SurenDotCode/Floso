Privacy Policy for Floso

Effective Date: July 2026

1. Overview

Floso ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. Floso is a productivity tool designed to capture and inject conversation context across supported AI platforms (claude.ai, chatgpt.com, and gemini.google.com).

2. Data Collection and Storage

Floso does not collect, transmit, track, or sell any personal data, telemetry, or conversation histories to external servers or third parties. All captured conversations ("capsules") are stored exclusively on your local device using Chrome's secure chrome.storage.local API.

3. Permissions Justification

  • Storage (chrome.storage.local): Used solely to save and manage your captured conversation capsules locally on your browser.
  • Host Permissions (claude.ai, chatgpt.com, gemini.google.com): Used strictly to run local content scripts that extract conversation text and inject context into message boxes on supported sites when explicitly triggered by you.

4. Data Control and Deletion

You maintain complete control over your data. You can expand, view, delete individual capsules, or clear your entire history at any time directly through the Floso extension popup window.
